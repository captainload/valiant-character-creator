Valiant Adventures RPG Quick Guide

The Engine: Valiant Adventures is powered by Green Ronin's Mutants & Masterminds (3rd Edition) d20 system. All resolution rolls use a single d20 + modifiers vs. a target Difficulty Class (DC).

Step-by-Step Character Creation

  1. Select Power Level (PL): The default is PL 10 with 150 Power Points (PP). Street-level gritty campaigns operate at PL 8 (120 PP), while cosmic threats reach PL 12+ (180+ PP).
  2. Core Abilities: Buy ranks for STR, STA, AGL, DEX, FGT, INT, AWE, PRE at 2 PP per rank. Ranks range from 0 (average adult human) to 5 (peak human) and 10+ (godlike).
  3. Defenses: Dodge (AGL), Parry (FGT), Fortitude (STA), Toughness (STA), and Will (AWE) can be increased at 1 PP per rank. Keep in mind: Dodge + Toughness ≤ 2 × PL, and Parry + Toughness ≤ 2 × PL.
  4. Skills: Purchase specialized proficiencies at 1 PP per 2 ranks (0.5 PP each). Total skill bonus equals Ability modifier + trained ranks.
  5. Advantages: Combat tactics, social perks, and fortune feats cost 1 PP per rank. The Equipment advantage grants 5 Equipment Points per rank.
  6. Powers: Craft unique superhuman or cybernetic abilities using base effects, customized with Extras (+PP/rank) and Flaws (-PP/rank).
  7. Gear: Spend your Equipment Points on armor, weapons, vehicles, and secure headquarters.
