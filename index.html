📖 Valiant Adventures RPG & App Comprehensive Guide

Version 1.6.36 • Mutants & Masterminds 3e Engine

Welcome to the Valiant Adventures RPG Character Creator & Virtual Tabletop Session Manager. Powered by Green Ronin's renowned Mutants & Masterminds (3rd Edition) ruleset, all resolution checks use a single d20 + Trait Modifiers vs. a Difficulty Class (DC). This guide explains all features of the application, especially automated combat logic, condition-based dice handling, and interactive session tools.

Bonus Die & Penalty Die Mechanics

Situational advantage/disadvantage resolution & automatic context detection
Core Rule: Instead of flat modifiers, significant tactical circumstances or status conditions introduce Bonus Dice or Penalty Dice.
  • Bonus Die (Advantage): Roll two d20s and use the higher result. (e.g., rolling 16 and 7 keeps 16).
  • Penalty Die (Disadvantage): Roll two d20s and use the lower result. (e.g., rolling 16 and 7 keeps 7).
  • Rule of Cancellation: If opposing bonus and penalty dice apply to the same check, they completely neutralize each other on a 1-to-1 basis, leaving a standard single d20 roll!
⚡ Less Obvious Feature: Intelligent Automatic Detection

When the die selector is left in Auto mode (the default), the app actively tracks your hero's status conditions, defensive powers, and combat state to determine if a Bonus or Penalty Die applies without requiring manual math:

⚠️ Auto-Penalty Disabled Condition

Having the Disabled condition automatically applies a Penalty Die to all checks made across the Session tab.

⚠️ Auto-Penalty Prone Condition (Close Attacks)

Being Prone automatically applies a Penalty Die to Close Attacks (e.g., Fighting, Close Combat skills, or melee powers). Ranged checks remain unaffected.

⚠️ Auto-Penalty Vulnerable Condition (Defenses)

The Vulnerable condition automatically applies a Penalty Die to active defense checks (Dodge and Parry) in the Incoming Attack Calculator.

⭐ Auto-Bonus Resistance Powers vs. Descriptors

If your hero has a power with the Resistance effect (e.g. Resistance: Fire), incoming attacks with that descriptor automatically grant a Bonus Die on the resistance check!

⭐ Auto-Bonus Improved Overwhelm

Characters with the Improved Overwhelm advantage automatically receive a Bonus Die when attempting an Overwhelm check.

⚖️ Auto-Cancel Opposing Modifiers Neutralized

If you are Disabled (-Die) but resist an attack covered by your Resistance power (+Die), the app automatically cancels them out to a standard check!

Manual Overrides: The Session top row provides Auto, Normal, + Bonus, and - Penalty buttons. If the GM grants a situational bonus (e.g. high ground or surprise attack), simply tap + Bonus to override for that check. Clicking an active manual button toggles it back to Auto.

Incoming Attack & Defense Calculator

Resolving incoming enemy attacks, automatic immunity, impervious thresholds, and degrees of failure

🎯 Base Difficulty Class (DC) Formulas

The calculator automatically configures the exact DC based on the defense trait rolled:

  • Toughness (Damage): DC = 15 + Incoming Attack Rank. (e.g. Rank 8 damage requires a Toughness check against DC 23).
  • All Other Defenses: Dodge, Parry, Fortitude, and Will use DC = 10 + Incoming Attack Rank. (e.g. Rank 8 affliction requires DC 18).

🛡️ Automated Immunity & Impervious Absorption

No manual bookkeeping required when enemy attacks hit your defensive powers:

  • Immunity: Attacks matching your Immunity power descriptors are completely bypassed with zero damage and an instant immunity confirmation.
  • Impervious Toughness: If the incoming damage rank is ≤ ceil(Impervious Rank / 2), the attack is completely absorbed with zero damage.
💥 4 Degrees of Damage Failure & Automated Status Transitions

Failing a Toughness check applies progressive damage according to standard M&M 3e rules:

1st Degree (-1 to -5)

+1 Bruise (-1 cumulative penalty to all future Toughness checks).

2nd Degree (-6 to -10)

Dazed (only 1 action) + 1 Bruise.

3rd Degree (-11 to -15)

Staggered (dazed & hindered) + 1 Bruise. Auto-Escalation: If already Staggered, automatically escalates to Incapacitated!

4th Degree (-16 or more)

Incapacitated (unconscious, defenseless) + 1 Bruise.

Hero Points (HP) & Targeted Re-Rolls

Spending fortune points, card lockout, and automatic defense damage rollback

🎯 Targeted Re-Roll (1–10 becomes 11–20)

Selecting Re-roll Last Check from the Spend HP dropdown targets the most recent check made (trait check, freeform dice roll, or defense check):

  • Card Lockout: All roller cards and defense buttons are temporarily locked out to prevent accidental duplicate clicks.
  • Apply & Cancel Buttons: The HP bar reveals Apply and Cancel buttons, displaying your prior roll value.
  • M&M 3e Re-Roll Rule: The die is re-rolled. Any result of 1–10 has +10 added to it (giving 11–20). The hero keeps the higher of the original roll vs. the new roll!

⏪ Less Obvious: Automatic Damage Rollback

When you spend a Hero Point to re-roll a Defense Check that originally failed, the app automatically reverts the previous failure's consequences (undoing the added bruise and clearing conditions like Dazed or Staggered) before resolving the new check! This ensures you never suffer duplicate damage from a re-rolled attack.

All Supported Hero Point Spends:

Combat Round Tracking & Automated Regeneration

Round advancement, interval tracking, and automated healing
🩸 Less Obvious Feature: Automated Regeneration

If your hero has a power with the Regeneration effect (such as Bloodshot's nanite bloodstream), clicking the Round +1 button tracks elapsed rounds and calculates your healing interval (10 / Rank rounds):

  • At the interval threshold, the app automatically heals 1 Bruise or removes a damage condition (Dazed, Staggered), updating your character state and displaying a confirmation toast notification.
  • Temporary condition durations and fading effects advance automatically round by round.
  • Clicking the R# round badge allows you to reset the encounter counter back to Round 1 at any time.

Session Dice Roller & Discord Broadcasting

Interactive cards, freeform syntax, roll history, and live webhooks

🃏 Click-to-Roll Trait Cards

Click any trait card in Combat & Defenses, Abilities, Skills, or Powers to roll. The result incorporates your trait rank and any value in Total Adj. Hover over any card for a full tooltip calculation breakdown. Inactive power array effects are visually dimmed.

Character Creation & Power Level Limits

Point budgets, trade-offs, and effective size modifiers
Power Level (PL) Budget: Characters receive 15 × PL Power Points (PP). The standard campaign default is PL 10 with 150 PP. Street-level campaigns operate at PL 8 (120 PP), while world-class heroes reach PL 12 (180 PP) or cosmic PL 14+ (210+ PP).

⚖️ Active Trade-Off Limits

The builder validates your traits in real-time against strict M&M 3e campaign limits:

  • Dodge + Toughness ≤ 2 × PL
  • Parry + Toughness ≤ 2 × PL
  • Fortitude + Will ≤ 2 × PL
  • Attack Bonus + Effect Rank ≤ 2 × PL

Warning badges display when limits are exceeded to keep characters rules-legal.

📐 Size Class & Effective Size

Characters have a base size (default Medium). If your hero purchases size-altering powers such as Growth or Shrinking, the builder automatically computes your Effective Size Class, adjusting your stealth modifiers, reach, speed, and active defense bonuses dynamically.

🗣️ Languages Advantage & Fluency Slots

All heroes are fluent in one Native Language for free. Each rank in the Languages advantage doubles the number of additional fluent languages known (Rank 1 = 1, Rank 2 = 2, Rank 3 = 4, Rank 4 = 8, Rank 5 = 16, etc.). The builder creates a dedicated slot under the advantage for every language unlocked by your ranks.

Character Builder Tab

Abilities, Defenses, Skills & Specialties, Advantages & Languages, Powers Builder, Gear, and Complications

💪 Abilities (2 PP per Rank)

Strength, Agility, Fighting, Awareness, Stamina, Dexterity, Intellect, and Presence. Each rank costs 2 PP and establishes baseline values for defenses, skills, carrying weight, and initiative.

🛡️ Defenses & PL Limits

Dodge, Parry, Fortitude, Toughness, and Will. The builder automatically verifies official Power Level limits: Dodge + Toughness ≤ 2×PL, Parry + Toughness ≤ 2×PL, and Fortitude + Will ≤ 2×PL.

🎯 Skills & Specialties (1 PP per 2 Ranks)

Standard skills can be used trained or untrained. The "+ Add Specialty" button allows adding focused sub-skills (e.g. Close Combat: Swords, Ranged Combat: Pistols, Expertise: Science) with independent rank adjustments. Default skills are never automatically added to new heroes.

🗣️ Skill Mastery & Languages

Skill Mastery is an unranked advantage that can be taken multiple times, each applying to a distinct skill that permits routine checks via an allowed skills dropdown. Languages grants 1 native language free, and each purchased rank doubles fluent languages known ($2^{\text{rank}-1}$).

⚡ Powers Builder & Descriptors

Select from standard effects (Damage, Affliction, Protection, Flight, Senses, etc.). Use the categorized dropdown with +add and -del buttons to manage descriptors. Apply flat modifiers and per-rank extras/flaws to dynamically compute net point costs.

🎒 Gear, Equipment & Complications

Purchase standard and custom weapons, vehicles, and headquarters using Equipment Points (1 PP = 5 EP). Define complications and motivations to ground your hero in the story and earn Hero Points during sessions.

Session Tab (Virtual Tabletop Combat Tracker)

Live combat management, Bruises, Conditions, Auto-penalties, Incoming Attack Calculator, Dice Roller, Hero Point spending, and Fading powers

💔 Bruises & Damage Tracking

Each failed damage check inflicts a Bruise. Every bruise imposes a cumulative -1 penalty to all subsequent Toughness checks. Quick stepper buttons allow incrementing, decrementing, or clearing bruises.

⚠️ Condition Row & Clear All

Located at the top of the Incoming Attack block. Displays active conditions (Dazed, Staggered, Incapacitated, Vulnerable, Defenseless, Prone, Disabled, Impaired). Click × on any badge to remove it, or click Clear All to remove all conditions and bruises simultaneously.

🛡️ Incoming Attack & Defense Calculator

Enter incoming attack rank or effect DC, select the defense trait, and resolve checks with a single click. The calculator automatically checks Impervious Toughness thresholds, subtracts bruises, and reports exact degrees of success or failure.

🎲 Interactive Dice Roller

Roll d20 checks using Auto, Normal, + Bonus Die (roll 2 pick higher), - Penalty Die (roll 2 pick lower), or Routine (take 10) modes. Natural 20s and Natural 1s receive distinct styling and are broadcast to Discord and the GM log.

⭐ Hero Point Tactical Spends

Spend Hero Points to Re-roll checks (rolls 1-10 boosted to 11-20), Recover 1 Bruise, Recover/Improve Conditions, gain an Extra Action or Power Stunt (Heroic Effort), Edit Scene, Escape Death, or gain Inspiration.

⏳ Fading Powers Countdown

Powers with the Fades flaw automatically track remaining activation rounds. Advancing a round decrements fading counters and broadcasts status updates to Discord and connected party members.

Incoming Attack & Defense Calculator Deep Dive

Resolving incoming enemy attacks, automatic immunity, impervious thresholds, and degrees of failure
Toughness Damage Resolution:

Damage checks use $\text{DC} = 15 + \text{Damage Rank}$. Rolling equal or higher resists all damage unharmed. Each failure threshold applies progressive consequences:

  • 1 Degree Fail (by 1-5): Inflicts 1 Bruise (-1 Toughness penalty).
  • 2 Degrees Fail (by 6-10): Inflicts 1 Bruise + Dazed (lasts 1 round).
  • 3 Degrees Fail (by 11-15): Inflicts 1 Bruise + Staggered (impaired speed/action).
  • 4+ Degrees Fail (by 16+): Character is Incapacitated (unconscious / defeated).
Automatic Immunity & Impervious:

• Impervious Defense: Attacks with a rank equal to or less than half your Impervious rank (rounded up) automatically fail to penetrate your defenses without rolling.
• Descriptor Immunity: If your hero has purchased an Immunity advantage or power against the incoming descriptor (e.g. Immunity: Fire), incoming attacks with that descriptor are ignored completely!

Hero Points & Tactical Spending

Earning Hero Points through complications and full tactical spending options

🎲 Re-Roll Check (+10 to 1-10)

Re-roll any check. Results between 1 and 10 automatically add +10 (e.g. rolling 4 becomes 14), guaranteeing a result of 11 to 20! If re-rolling a defense check, prior damage is automatically reverted.

🩹 Recover Bruise or Condition

Spend a Hero Point to remove 1 bruise (-1 Toughness penalty cleared), clear the Dazed or Fatigued condition, or improve Staggered to Dazed and Incapacitated to Staggered.

⚡ Heroic Effort & Narrative Action

Spend a Hero Point for an Extra Action this turn, perform a temporary Power Stunt, introduce a favorable narrative element (Edit Scene), or automatically Escape Death from a fatal blow.
